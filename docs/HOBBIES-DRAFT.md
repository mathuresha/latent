# Hobby layer: draft suggestions (Indian starting points)

These are drafts for you and Vinay to curate. They are already wired into the app
(`src/data/hobbies.js`) and show only for results in the top 30%. **Please verify every class, group
and tool before launch**; I could not check local availability or links.

Framing rule from the plan: a head start, never destiny.

## Pitch
| Hobby | Why it fits | Start this week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hindustani / Carnatic vocals | Staying on a swara is fine pitch control | Trial class with a guru; sa-re-ga-ma against a tanpura app, 10 min a day |
| Violin or another fretless instrument | No frets, so the ear does the tuning | Rent or borrow one; open strings, then a scale |
| Music production by ear | Hearing small errors in a note or mix | Free tool (BandLab, GarageBand); rebuild a 4-bar loop by ear |

More ideas: choir or a cappella group, learning a tonal language (Mandarin), piano tuning, sound engineering.

## Visual memory
| Hobby | Why it fits | Start this week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chess | Hold a board in mind, notice what changed | 10 min a day on lichess.org, one puzzle a day |
| Speedcubing | Track many pieces and plan ahead | Basic cube, beginner-method tutorial, aim for under 2 minutes |
| Memory palace | Built on strong visual memory | 10 images along a familiar route, recall tomorrow |

More ideas: contract bridge, photography, drawing from memory, board games.

## Mental rotation
| Hobby | Why it fits | Start this week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3D modelling | Turning shapes in your head | Install Blender, model a mug from a tutorial |
| Bouldering | Reading a route means imagining body positions | Beginner session at an indoor gym |
| Perspective sketching | Drawing from a new angle is rotation on paper | Cube, stack of cubes, then a room you know, from three viewpoints |

More ideas: origami, CAD (for example Fusion), pottery, woodworking, 3D printing, drone flying.
